April 4th 2022 — Grand Opening Celebration

On April 4th, 2022, Pee-Pa's Garage Hatchet Barn opened its doors for the first time. After months of building, planning, and Jim Brown convincing his family this was a great idea — it was finally real.

The Hatchet Barn was the natural next step after Jim turned his automotive collision repair shop into Pee-Pa's Garage Craft Brewery. He had the space. He had the community. He had the vision. The only thing missing was axes flying through the air.

The Day Itself

Family. Friends. Regulars from the brewery. Curious neighbors who walked over to see what was happening. Pinellas Park hadn't seen anything like it.

By the end of the night, the lanes had seen hundreds of throws, the bar had poured countless pints, and Jim was already thinking about what came next.

Where We Are Now

Since opening, the Hatchet Barn has hosted bachelorette parties, corporate team-builds, league seasons, birthday throws, and one memorable visit from the Orange County Choppers crew.

We've added WATL and WKTL sanctioned league play. We've grown the team. And we're just getting started.

Thanks for being part of it.
