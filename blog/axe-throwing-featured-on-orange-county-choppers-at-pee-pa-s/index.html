Axe Throwing Featured on Orange County Choppers at Pee-Pa's

It's not every day the Orange County Choppers crew rolls into your hatchet barn. But that's exactly what happened at Pee-Pa's Garage when Paul Sr., Paul Jr., and the team stopped by for a session.

The Hatchet Barn shares a roof with Pee-Pa's Garage Craft Brewery — and with Jim Brown's classic-car-and-bikes garage culture, it made perfect sense to host one of the most iconic builder crews in motorcycle history.

What Happened

The crew tried their hand at our throwing lanes — some better than others, we'll leave it at that. There was beer. There was banter. There was a lot of laughing every time someone bounced an axe off the wood.

For Jim, it was the kind of day that ties everything together. The garage. The brewery. The hatchet barn. The community of people who like to build things, throw things, and drink something cold while doing both.
