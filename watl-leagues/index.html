Axe Throwing League Types 101

Discover the different leagues we offer at Pee-Pa's Garage Hatchet Barn, where every throw counts and every experience is unique!

🪓

What is Single Hatchet?

This is basic axe throwing league – perfect for all levels!

🪓

What is Big Axe?

The BIG axe throwing league features a larger axe that is thrown from a further distance. We require throwers to have at least one season experience (at our venue or another) before registering for this league. Have experience outside of the league? Swing by and talk to our team to see if we can make an exception.

⚔️

What is Duals?

In this axe throwing league, we have 2 people throwing at the same time at the same target – you should have some throwing experience before trying this as it can cause damage to your axe if it collides with your partner's axe.

🔪

What about Knives?

Please check back for our upcoming Knife League 2025.

Regulation Information

Everything you need to know about how our league season is structured.

How Long is a League Season?

Each season lasts 9 weeks.

  • • Weeks 1–7: Standard Play (4 games/night)
  • • Week 8: Make-Ups Night
  • • Week 9: Double Elimination Tournament

There are TWO categories you can win for 1st, 2nd and 3rd place! Yes, you can win in BOTH categories in the same league season.

Is there a place that I can check my standings?

Yes, there are! YoReferee is the official software of the World Axe Throwing League for scoring. You can check the data on their website.
