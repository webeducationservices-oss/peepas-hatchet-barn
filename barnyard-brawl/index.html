The Barnyard Brawl

Three days of sanctioned competition at Pee-Pa's Garage Hatchet Barn in Pinellas Park. Knives, Hatchet, Duals and Big Axe, with slots capped on every division. November 13 to 15, 2026.

Register Now See the Schedule

What the Brawl Is

The Barnyard Brawl is a local tournament sanctioned by the World Axe Throwing League and the World Knife Throwing League, hosted in our lanes over a single weekend. Six divisions run across three days, so you can enter one or stack several.

Every division has a hard slot cap, from 8 in Knife Duals up to 32 in the Hatchet and Big Axe brackets. When a bracket fills, it is closed, so register for the ones you want early rather than deciding at the door.

The brewery is pouring all weekend and the kitchen is open, so bring people to watch even if they are not throwing.
